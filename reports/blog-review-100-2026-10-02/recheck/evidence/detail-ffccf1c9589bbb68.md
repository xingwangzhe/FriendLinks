应 [xjb](http://www.watch-life.net) 邀请，我也弄了一个 [Google+](http://plus.google.com) 的帐号(注： 访问需穿墙或[更改 hosts 文件](https://www.xiaohui.com/weekly/google-plus-hosts.htm))。玩了两天，感觉比 Twitter 和 Facebook 要好。最大的亮点就是“圈子”的功能，这样可以在不同的群体中隔离不同的信息。另外这个产品继承了 Google 惯有的简洁和易用，没有什么学习成本，比较对我的胃口。
我的地址： [https://plus.google.com/108323473136128442646](https://plus.google.com/108323473136128442646/)。欢迎圈我。需要邀请码的，请先确保你能翻墙，然后直接[点击此邀请链接](https://plus.google.com/_/notifications/ngemlink?path=%2F%3Fgpinv%3Db8Yqn0dBhNM%3ACcQzlGmMCtA)，打开之后，输入你的 Google 帐户即可。**请不要再留下 Email 问我要邀请码** 。(更新于 2011.08.08)~~需要邀请码的可以[在评论中直接留下 gmail 地址](https://www.xiaohui.com/weekly/google-plus.htm#addreview)。~~
目前 Google 并没有开放 Google+ 的搜索功能。想要搜索 Google+ 联系人或贴子，可以访问第三方网站 [Google Plus Search](http://googleplussearch.chromefans.org/)，支持中文。
Google 的大多数服务，例如邮件、 Gtalk、Adsense等，我都是用的 Google Apps 帐号。有点遗憾的是，不能用 Google Apps 的帐号登录 Google+。具体原因，Google 的解释是因为 Google+ 要依赖 Google Profile 服务。而 Google Profile 服务 并没有开放给 Google Apps。
但据 [Goole Forum 上的讨论](http://www.google.com/support/forum/p/Google+Apps/thread?tid=08f56168a00dc731&hl=en)以及[Google Official Blog](http://googleenterprise.blogspot.com/2011/03/coming-soon-to-google-apps-1-button-and.html) 说，需要过几个月 Google Apps 将支持 Google+。若是真的话，还要等这么久，也太不给力了:
> ## Coming soon to Google Apps: +1 button and Google Profiles 
> Last year we made more than 60 additional Google services available in Google Apps, including Blogger, YouTube and Picasa. Soon we’ll be bringing two of the newest Google features to our Google Apps customers: enhanced Google Profiles and the +1 button.
> Google Profiles is an important companion to +1’s, but your profile is also a place for you to manage your online identity. Once it’s ready, Google Profiles can be turned on by your organization's administrator. You might choose to use a Google Profile if you’re a freelance writer, personal accountant or hairstylist, for instance, and you want to create your professional presence on the web to interact with potential clients and colleagues. Your profile will be public, so anyone on the web will be able to view it and your profile and +1’s will show up in search results. 
> ## Will Google Plus be available for Google Apps users? 
> FaganSC: From all the posts I've been reading on Google Plus for Google Apps users, Google Profiles (and Google Plus) should be coming to Apps users soon. It sounds like Google wants to roll this out to there EDU Google Apps users first, which tell me that this is something they would want to do this summer before schools start up again in the fall. Hopefully the use of Google Profiles and Google Plus rolls out soon to us Google Apps users, since my Apps account is my primary personal account and I'm trying to not use my old Gmail account for stuff anymore. I really don't want to fire up my old Gmail account and create a Google Plus account to check out this new system. I would really like to keep all my stuff under my Personal Google Apps Account.
