## About
![Cara](https://caramiki.com/images/me2_600x600.jpg)
My name is Cara Michele Ryan (née Heacock). I am a web developer, graphic designer, and illustrator currently living in Omaha, Nebraska.
My hobbies include making websites for fun, board games, drawing, writing fantasy stories, playing acoustic guitar and bass, and generally creating things and being creative. Currently I have a [day job](https://www.unabridgedsoftware.com/), [a side company](http://waterbeargames.com/) that I run with my cousin, and other [side projects](http://sideprojectsclub.com/).
