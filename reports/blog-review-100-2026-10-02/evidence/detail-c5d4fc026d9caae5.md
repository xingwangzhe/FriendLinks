#  [](https://blog.imz.me/posts/julipy/#%E5%AE%89%E8%A3%85VScode "安装VScode")安装VScode
##  [](https://blog.imz.me/posts/julipy/#%E5%89%8D%E5%BE%80Vscode%E5%AE%98%E7%BD%91%E4%B8%8B%E8%BD%BD "前往Vscode官网下载")前往Vscode官网下载
点击前往Vscode官网
[![](https://blog.imz.me/img/loading.avif)](https://pic.imgdb.cn/item/671c5aa0d29ded1a8cc042da.png)  
点击安装Windows版本
##  [](https://blog.imz.me/posts/julipy/#%E8%AE%BE%E7%BD%AE%E4%B8%AD%E6%96%87-%E5%8F%AF%E9%80%89%EF%BC%8C%E4%B8%8D%E5%BD%B1%E5%93%8D%E5%90%8E%E7%BB%AD%E6%93%8D%E4%BD%9C "设置中文\(可选，不影响后续操作\)")设置中文(可选，不影响后续操作)
安装完成之后打开Vscode主界面  
点击左侧**插件** ，在输入框中输入**Chinese** ，选择**Chinese (Simplified) (简体中文) Language Pack for Visual Studio Code**  
[![](https://blog.imz.me/img/loading.avif)](https://pic.imgdb.cn/item/671c5c46d29ded1a8cc1c828.png)
##  [](https://blog.imz.me/posts/julipy/#%E5%AE%89%E8%A3%85Python%E6%8F%92%E4%BB%B6 "安装Python插件")安装Python插件
在输入框中输入**python** ，安装下图所示两个模块  
[![](https://blog.imz.me/img/loading.avif)](https://pic.imgdb.cn/item/671c5d5dd29ded1a8cc2836b.png)
#  [](https://blog.imz.me/posts/julipy/#%E8%BF%90%E8%A1%8C%E7%A8%8B%E5%BA%8F "运行程序")运行程序
在D盘中新建一个目录(可自行取名，我的叫做air-py)  
_**将juli3.py复制到该目录下**_  
点击**VScode** 左上角的**文件** ，选择**打开文件夹** ，选择刚刚创建的目录  
[![](https://blog.imz.me/img/loading.avif)](https://pic.imgdb.cn/item/671c5df9d29ded1a8cc2fc97.png)  
点开juli3.py，点击右上角的**运行** ，就可以了  
[![](https://blog.imz.me/img/loading.avif)](https://pic.imgdb.cn/item/671c5e62d29ded1a8cc35bc0.png)
