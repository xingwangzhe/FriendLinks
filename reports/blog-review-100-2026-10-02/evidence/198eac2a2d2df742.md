```
To keep dreams alive.
存续梦想。

To preserve life.
延续生命。

That alone is reason enough.
仅此，便已足够。

To heal, one must cut.

欲使伤口愈合。

便需将其剖开。

To obtain, one must relinquish.

欲有所获。

便需有所舍。

To survive, one must wound another.

欲求生存。

便难免伤及他人。

None of these are justice.

它们并非正义。

Nor are they evil.

亦非邪恶。

They are merely choices.

只是选择。

Life longs to live.

生命渴望存续。

It reaches for tomorrow.

因此伸手触及明天。

It fears farewell.

因此惧怕离别。

Even knowing the price.

纵然知晓代价。

It still refuses to stop.

亦不会停下脚步。

I have seen hatred save lives.

我见过憎恶救人。

I have seen kindness take lives.

也见过善意害人。

Mercy and cruelty

仁慈与残忍，

are sometimes separated

有时，

only by the hand that holds the blade.

不过执刃之人的一念之间。

Medicine cannot cure desire.

药剂无法医治欲望。

Nor can it erase regret.

也无法抹去遗憾。

It merely grants

它所给予的，

another chance

不过是，

to choose.

再一次选择的机会。

If one day,

若终有一日，

my name is forgotten,

我的名字无人记得。

my body returns to dust,

我的身体归于尘土。

my voice falls silent,

我的声音消散于世。

Then let what I could not finish

愿我未能完成之事，

be carried on

仍有人，

by another.

继续前行。

I do not mourn my death.

我并不为自己的死亡悲伤。

Only for those

我只是遗憾，

who still waited

那些仍在等待的人。

The wish to save another

拯救他人的欲望，

was never a flower.

从来不是花。

It was always

它只是，

a seed.

一粒种子。

Buried quietly,

静静埋入泥土。

Forgotten.

无人知晓。

Until the day

直到生命终结之时，

it blooms

才终于，

through another life.

在另一人的生命里盛开。
```
