# Making Some Donations
By Fenguoerbian in [Linux](https://fenguoerbian.netlify.app/categories/linux) [Open Source](https://fenguoerbian.netlify.app/categories/open-source) [Donation](https://fenguoerbian.netlify.app/categories/donation)
March 30, 2025
This is not my first time making a donation but the first time I’m making donations to open source communities. So this post is a small record of the projects I donate to:
  * [The R Project](https://r-project.org)
  * [Gnome, the desktop environment](https://www.gnome.org/)
  * [KDE, the desktop environment](https://kde.org/)
  * [Tex User Group](https://www.tug.org/)
  * [Thunderbird, the email client](https://www.thunderbird.net/en-US/)


These projects have been very helpful in my life. Though the amount of my donation is small, I hope all these projects can keep on going, thrive and prosper.
