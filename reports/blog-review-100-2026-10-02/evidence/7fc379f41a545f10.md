## [Reading notes on Naming Things by Tom Benner](https://masalmon.eu/2026/08/27/naming-things-reading-notes/)
2026/08/27
[good practice](https://masalmon.eu/tags/good-practice) / [books](https://masalmon.eu/tags/books)
[View source](https://github.com/maelle/simplymaelle/blob/master/content/post/2026-08-27-naming-things-reading-notes/index.md)
Naming Things by Tom Benner a tiny but neat book about the naming of identifies in code (variables, classes, methods, so not packages or libraries). It had entered my to-read list a few years ago, when I read the blog post Naming Things by Vicki Boykis. Tom Benner’s book is self published and available from Amazon (e-book and paperback) and Leanpub (e-book). I rarely buy stuff on Amazon, and prefer to read on paper, so I patiently waited for a copy to appear on my favorite second-hand book website.

## How to pronounce my name
My last name is Salmon like the fish and the colour, and you can pronounce it like that if you want. My first name, Maëlle, is derived from a Celtic word that means princess or chief. It’s pronounced “Ma-El” as “ma” in “macaroni” and as the Spanish “El”.
