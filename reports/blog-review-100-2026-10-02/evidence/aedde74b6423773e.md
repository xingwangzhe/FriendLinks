## Recent Work
###  Nord Design System
Nordhealth hired me in 2020 to help reimagine what the company’s approach to designing, building and shipping digital products at scale should look like. During the next 5 years I have lead the vision, strategy, roadmap and implementation of Nord.
I started my work with [Nordhealth](https://nordhealth.com) by conducting a series of user research activities and workshops to reveal a lack of alignment and personal biases across business units, products and teams to understand how the design system could help them.
When we had a clear view of the challenges that we wanted to solve, it was a matter of creating fundamental principles and goals for the system that we could follow as a team. We came up with [four primary goals for Nord](https://nordhealth.design/about/#our-goals). At the same time, I also created [design principles](https://nordhealth.design/principles/) for the design systems work that would form the foundations for Nord and help us do better and more informed decisions.
Whenever I talk about [Nord Design System](https://arielsalminen.com/projects/nord/), one of the first things that I like to mention is that it’s tech agnostic, not tech specific. Meaning that you can take any component from Nord and use it with any framework. That could be Angular, React, Preact, Vue.js, plain HTML, your old PHP application, or any future framework. We achieved this by building on top of existing web standards _(Web Components to be exact),_ instead of choosing a specific framework to go with.
Nord’s tech-agnostic thinking isn’t limited to components either, all of the styles are agnostic as well. We used [design tokens](https://arielsalminen.com/projects/nord/tokens/) instead of hard coded values to ensure a better UI consistency across different platforms. Be that a web application, a native application, a mobile application, a VR solution, or something new that doesn’t even exist today.
Eventually, being very hands-on personally, I also designed and built the majority of the system’s infrastructure, APIs, [Web Components](https://arielsalminen.com/projects/nord/components/), Eleventy based documentation platform, [accessibility best practices](https://arielsalminen.com/projects/nord/accessibility-checklist/), and wrote most of the [system’s documentation](https://arielsalminen.com/projects/nord/).
Additionally, I created supporting design system tooling such as the [Nord Color Utilities](https://nordhealth.design/colors/#color-utilities) and the [Nord Color Generator](https://nordhealth.design/color-generator/) that generates color palettes program­matically for use with the Nord Themes. I also built a tool called [Nord Theme Builder](https://nordhealth.design/theme-builder/) for testing and manipulating the existing design system themes.
Today, [Nord Design System](https://arielsalminen.com/projects/nord/) is widely recognized in the tech industry and serves as Nordhealth’s design system for products, digital experiences and brand. It’s a collection of reusable components and tools, guided by clear standards, that can be assembled together to build digital products and experiences.
In 2022, I wrote about [launching Nord Design System](https://arielsalminen.com/2022/launching-nord-design-system/) and was more recently, in 2024, interviewed by the [Design Systems Guide](https://thedesignsystem.guide/interview-ariel-salminen) about this work. Nord Design System was eventually nominated for [Jamstack Conf Jammies award](https://arielsalminen.com/2022/finalist-for-the-jamstack-conf-jammies/) in addition to [Best Design System award](https://arielsalminen.com/2023/finalist-for-the-best-design-system-award/).
[Open project ](https://arielsalminen.com/projects/nord/)
###  Nordhealth
Between 2021 – 2023 I worked as the Head of Design and later as the CDO overseeing Nordhealth’s design operations and driving the company’s design transformation. We scaled a team of 1 to a team of 20 designers and frontend developers over this period.
I also redesigned [Nordhealth’s brand identity](https://nordhealth.design/brand/) from the ground up to mirror the growth and transformation of the company into the leading cloud-based provider of healthcare and veterinary software. As a result, “Three Plus Group” and “Finnish Net Solutions” today have a new name and an unified corporate identity called “Nordhealth.”
The rebranding I did was a start of a bigger design transformation in the company. We didn’t just merely rethink the corporate brand. We continued this work and I helped to re-imagine Nordhealth’s company values, design processes and development tools. Eventually this work also lead to us publishing the [Nord Design System](https://nordhealth.design/brand/).
In April, 2021, [I wrote a press release](https://arielsalminen.com/2021/nordhealth-rebranding/) about Nordhealth’s brand refresh which represented “togetherness.” Nordhealth’s new brand is today widely recognized in the industry and attracts skilled experts across the globe to work for them.
[Open project ](https://nordhealth.com/)
###  Duet Design System
I helped LocalTapiola establish their design systems infrastructure between 2018 and 2021. My foundational work on reinventing how LocalTapiola builds digital products lead to a big design trans­formation in the organization.
[LocalTapiola](https://arielsalminen.com/projects/duet/) is one of the biggest insurance groups in Finland. During the first months of working with this client I conducted design systems research and workshops, in addition to introducing new guidelines and approach for improving the accessibility and performance of their products.
Following this, I designed and built the foundational architecture, design token system, CSS framework, icon library, testing automation, and most of their web components. I also designed and developed a [custom documentation platform](https://arielsalminen.com/projects/duet/), design system analytics tools, and a command line interface for the system.
In 2019, I wrote an [article on why we use web components](https://arielsalminen.com/2019/why-we-use-web-components/) in the Duet Design System. In 2020, I built a set of extensions for the system that made it possible to render the code based components in design tools, getting rid of the need to maintain separate design and code libraries.
Ultimately, my foundational work on the [new design tools](https://arielsalminen.com/2017/design-tools-processes/) lead to a big design transformation in the whole organization.
[Open project ](https://arielsalminen.com/projects/duet/)
###  Nosto Design System
Nosto hired me in 2017 to run a series of design sprints to create solid foundations for their design system. I also built a custom documen­tation platform for Nosto which was later published as an open source tool called Vue Design System.
During the first four weeks of this assign­ment I pulled Nosto’s design systems team together in the same physical space to focus on different product features to redesign utilizing the design principles and design tokens we established.
The principles and [draft styleguide](https://arielsalminen.com/2018/vue-design-system/#drafting-type%2C-color-%26-spacing) created during the first week guided our work into a unified direction while allow­ing room for exploration and discovery. At the end of each day we reviewed, discussed, and spent time examining how different patterns started to emerge.
In 2017, the design system tooling for [Vue](https://arielsalminen.com/2018/vue-design-system/) wasn’t great, which is why I also built a custom documentation platform for this client that was later published as an open source tool called [Vue Design System](https://vueds.com). In 2018, I [wrote about Vue Design System](https://arielsalminen.com/2018/vue-design-system/) and how we used the tool at Nosto.
[Open project ](https://vueds.com)
